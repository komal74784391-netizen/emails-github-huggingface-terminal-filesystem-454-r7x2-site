Ground-level sensor networks in hazy regions are sparse and frequently drop out.
We introduce a gap-aware ConvGRU/transformer hybrid that ingests irregular
station observations together with satellite aerosol fields and forecasts
PM2.5 at 6, 24 and 48 hour horizons.
